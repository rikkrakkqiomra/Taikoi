Six months, at least

The public face of a company is not a logo dropped onto a template. It is the site, the mark, the way a page is composed, and every quiet failure underneath. We take that on as a continuous engagement: half a year at the shortest, and longer when the work asks for it.

Philosophical and Technological Mind Expansion

Four months, or eight

Vision fails when it is only a sentence on a wall. This is a working course in how to think one, and how to act while the conditions keep moving. We meet when the weeks allow, not on a rigid timetable, and the work between meetings is part of the course.

The reading is realistic. The history of philosophy and of psychology, as those traditions actually ran, set next to systems thinking as it is used now. No mystique in place of a method. When the months end, the materials stay with you, and the right to use them is yours.

Sovereign AI Intelligence Architecture

Pure AI

An intelligence that lives where you do. We plant a private, local system in the environment you name: weights on your metal, inference in your rooms, no open internet unless you open a door. The platform is model-agnostic. The model is a guest. The house is yours.

Cloud intelligence is a lease. The CLOUD Act and FISA 702 do not stop at a European data centre; extra-territorial reach is the point. A hyperscaler's subprocessors, telemetry, and vendor lock-in are the architecture, not an accident. Schrems II already said the quiet part: a transfer is still a transfer.

The architecture is built for digital sovereignty and data residency. On-premises as a rule. Air-gapped if you ask. Data localisation that is actual localisation, not a sovereign-cloud sticker on someone else's rack. GDPR and NIS2 are easier to keep when the processing never left the building.

We walk the security, the isolation, and a plain interface. You leave able to run it without a translator in the room, and without a jurisdiction you did not invite.
