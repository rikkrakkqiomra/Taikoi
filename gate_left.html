Optimizing Perfusion Conditions for 3D Neurovascular Networks on Chip

A rocking platform was asked to give three-dimensional neurovascular cultures a living, bidirectional flow. Chips that shared one hydrogel, one medium, and one geometry — neurons alone, vessels of marrow stroma with endothelium, and the three together — did not outgrow their hydrostatic twins. Perfusion still matters. This particular motion did not.
