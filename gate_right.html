Folium II · apparatus sine operatore

Apparatus Without Operator

A machine that waits is already a theology.

The brass lung was assembled from three centuries. No one claimed the crank. In the margin a later hand begins “when the operator—”

Folium III · septimum deest

The Missing Seventh

Six laws hold. The seventh is the silence they orbit.

Treatises on the solar engine name six motions. A burned quire once named a seventh that would have made the others honest.

Folium V · census mundorum nondum natorum

Census of Unborn Worlds

To count a world before it happens is a kind of theft.

Columns of names, then a crease, then numbers that skip from twelve to forty. The census-taker writes “still arriving” and underlines nothing.
